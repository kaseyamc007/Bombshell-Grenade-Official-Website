The Official Biography

THE FIRE BEHIND THE MIC

Bwalya Sophie Chibesakunda — The lyrical prodigy, university graduate, and continental award winner who redefined African female hip hop.

Official Birth Name

Bwalya Sophie Chibesakunda

Born 31 December 1987 • Lusaka, Zambia

Origins & Early Life

Born with Rhythm in Lusaka

Bwalya Sophie Chibesakunda was born and raised in the vibrant capital of Lusaka, Zambia. Surrounded by a household with eclectic musical taste, she was first introduced to the pulse of hip hop by her uncles. Mesmerized by the cadence, wordplay, and expressive storytelling of the genre, she started rapping at just 11 years old and began writing her own original songs at age 12.

Her early years were spent sharpening her pen, mastering rhyme schemes, and developing the explosive vocal delivery that would later inspire her stage name: Bomb$hell Grenade (often written simply as Bombshell).

Academic Excellence

Retail Business Management Degree

Unlike many artists whose paths diverge from formal education, Bombshell recognized the indispensable value of business mastery. She relocated to Cape Town, South Africa, to attend the prestigious Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT), successfully graduating with a degree in Retail Business Management.

This academic foundation would become her strategic weapon in negotiating entertainment contracts, launching corporate partnerships, and championing social enterprise initiatives across Africa.

Chronology of Triumph

Historic Career Milestones

From street cipher prodigy to continental queen.

BREAKTHROUGH

"The Berg" ft. Jay Rox

Bombshell first gained massive public recognition with the release of her single "The Berg" featuring celebrated Zambian star Jay Rox. The release cemented her position as a force to be reckoned with.

COLLABORATION

Kaladoshas — "They Don't Know"

Her distinct delivery and versatility reached widespread audiences when she provided commanding guest vocals on Kaladoshas' hit track "They Don't Know," dominating Southern African radio rotations.

DECEMBER 2016

"Shame Ol' Me" ft. Mumba Yachi

Releasing the soulful and introspective single "Shame Ol' Me" featuring folk maestro Mumba Yachi, demonstrating deep emotional range and critical songwriting maturity.

OCTOBER 2017

Kwacha Music Awards & MTV Base

Won Best Newcomer at the inaugural Kwacha Music Awards in Lusaka. That same year, international broadcast giant MTV Base ranked her among the Top Six Rappers in Africa.

2018 - 2019

"Jump Off" & "Backshot" Phenomenon

Released "Jump Off" in November 2018, followed by her 2019 blockbuster single "Backshot", officially named one of the year's top hit singles by The Lusaka Star.

2020

Urban Girl Social Advocacy

Partnered with reusable sanitary pad brand Urban Girl to fight period poverty and promote menstrual hygiene equity for underserved schoolgirls.

2021 AFRIMMA

Best Female Rapper in Africa

Crowned the AFRIMMA Best Female Rapper in Africa at the prestigious African Muzik Magazine Awards, lifting the Zambian flag to continental supremacy.

2022 - PRESENT

Coronation of Mfumukazi ("A Female King") & Debut LP

Released her 19-track magnum opus album Mfumu Kadzi and formally enshrined her royal title Mfumukazi — meaning "A Female King or female royal ruler" — cementing her supreme sovereign reign across African music and culture.
